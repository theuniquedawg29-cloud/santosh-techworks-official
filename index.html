Frequently Asked Questions

What does this app do?

It covers all the chapters included in the Quantitative Aptitude or Math syllabus of any competitive exams, breaking down each content into easy digestible bite-sized modules along with practice sets and solved common complex questions which appeared in exams with detailed explanations, diagrams, and an AI assistant to guide you 24/7 through your learning journey.

How does this help in learning?

The app has a gamified coin logic where rewards are given for every correct answer and completing tutorial modules. Coins are charged for wrong answers and for exploring features like analyzing previous answersheets to understand mistakes or asking extra AI prompts. This creates an immersive exam simulation that boosts engagement and skills through detailed mistake analysis.

Is it better than other apps like Testbook or other educational apps?

Well, those apps indeed are great choices as they are created by highly experienced professionals, so they are definitely good resources. But if you are looking for something completely free and a 24/7 learning companion, then Quant-Elite is the one you should choose.
