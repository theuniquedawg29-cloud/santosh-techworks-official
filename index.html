Common Questions

Frequently Asked.

How fast can you actually deliver?

Small, focused tools ship in about 15 days. Full products with custom backends typically take 30 days. You'll get an exact timeline in writing after the discovery call, before any work starts.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short call or written brief describing the problem you're solving, your target users, and any must-have features. I'll turn that into a scoped proposal within 24 hours.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a post-launch support window for fixes, and the Ongoing Partner plan covers continuous feature work and priority response after that.

Can you work with schools or coaching institutes on a limited budget?

Yes — see the Mission: Education program above. I offer reduced-cost and, in select cases, free collaboration with educational institutes in exchange for feedback and support in refining the tools.

Who actually writes the code?

I do, personally, end to end — discovery, design, development, testing, and deployment. No outsourcing and no handoffs between teams.

How do payments work?

Projects are split into milestones tied to the agreed scope, typically an upfront deposit followed by payments at defined checkpoints. Full terms are confirmed in writing before work begins.
