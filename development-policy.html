Development & Service Policy

This policy outlines the terms, conditions, and professional standards followed by Santosh TechWorks for custom software development, automation, and consulting services.

1. Scope of Service

I provide rapid-delivery digital solutions including Mobile Apps (Flutter/Dart), Web Platforms (React/Node), Custom Desktop Software, and Task Automation (Python/VBA). All projects are initiated with a fixed "Solution Blueprint" that defines the exact scope.

2. Flexible Pricing & Negotiation

At Santosh TechWorks, I prioritize value over rigid pricing structures:

  • Negotiability: All rates stated on the portfolio are baseline estimates and are fully negotiable.
  • Complexity Adjustment: Final quotes may increase or decrease based on the actual technical complexity, hours required, and specific integration needs.
  • Payment Milestones: Projects are typically billed in stages (Discovery, Prototype, Final Delivery) as agreed upon in the project contract.

3. Delivery Timeline Guarantee

I am committed to providing the fastest possible service in the independent market:

  • Rapid Tools: Delivered within 15 days.
  • Large Systems: Delivered within a maximum of 30 days.
  • Timelines are finalized during the Blueprint stage and strictly adhered to unless the client requests scope changes mid-project.

4. Mission: Education Collaboration

I offer special terms for educational institutes:

  • Affordability: Services are provided at the lowest possible cost to help modernize learning.
  • Free Services: Select tools may be provided free of charge, subject to conditions such as app recommendations or collaborative favors.

5. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon final payment, the client receives full ownership of the production-ready code and assets developed specifically for their project, unless otherwise specified in a retainer agreement.
