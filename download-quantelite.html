Who can use Quant-Elite?

Quant-Elite is suitable for beginners, school students, college students, job aspirants and anyone who wants additional practice in Mathematics or Logical Reasoning.

Can beginners use Quant-Elite?

Yes. Beginners can start with foundational topics, guided explanations and basic practice before moving to more difficult questions and timed challenges.

Can school students use Quant-Elite?

School students can use the app to strengthen arithmetic, algebra, geometry, percentages, ratios, data interpretation and other core mathematical skills.

Is it useful for SSC preparation?

Quant-Elite supports SSC-style preparation with quantitative aptitude, Logical Reasoning, chapter practice, previous-year questions, formula revision and timed practice.

Can Banking aspirants practise speed?

Yes. Banking examinations require accuracy and speed. Timed quizzes, calculation challenges and topic-based practice can help you build a consistent solving routine.

Does the app cover Logical Reasoning?

The learning experience includes reasoning-focused practice such as patterns, arrangements, classifications, series, puzzles and other commonly practised reasoning concepts.

What if I do not understand a solution?

You can use Ganitacharya AI to ask for clarification and request a step-by-step explanation. The aim is to help you understand the method instead of only showing the final answer.

Can I use it for CAT, NDA or Railways?

Students preparing for CAT, NDA, Railways and similar examinations can use Quant-Elite for Mathematics, aptitude, reasoning, revision and timed practice. Your preparation should also include the latest official syllabus and pattern.

Does Quant-Elite replace a teacher?

No. Quant-Elite is a supplementary learning tool. Students may still benefit from teachers, coaching, books and official examination resources.

Can I practise with limited daily time?

Yes. You can use short practice sessions, quick calculation challenges or a single chapter revision session. Consistency is often easier to maintain than irregular, lengthy sessions.

How should I start my preparation?

Start by identifying your target examination, assess your current level, revise weak concepts, solve topic-based questions and gradually add timed quizzes and full-length tests.

How can I request new topics or report an issue?

Use the Feedback page to suggest chapters, report incorrect content, request resources or share problems with the app experience.
