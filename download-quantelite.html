How does the Ganitacharya AI Voice & Camera solver work?

Simply snap a clear photo of any math or reasoning question or speak into the microphone. Ganitacharya AI reads the question and provides instant, step-by-step explanations, diagrams, and formula breakdowns powered by Google Gemini AI.

What is Quant Runner and how does it help speed math?

Quant Runner is an action-packed 2D runner game where you control a runner by solving rapid mental math calculations under time pressure. It trains your brain to calculate faster, dodge mistakes, earn coins, and build calculation confidence.

Does Quant-Elite work offline?

Yes. Core offline scripts, interactive chapter widgets, formula cheat sheets, and offline practice modules work smoothly without an active internet connection.

What is Quiz Clash?

Quiz Clash is a real-time multiplayer and AI timed speed battle arena where you compete head-to-head against peers to test accuracy, speed, and composure under authentic exam conditions.

What are VivaZone and AI Portfolio Analytics?

VivaZone conducts interactive concept verification Q&A to check true understanding over rote memorization. AI Portfolio Analytics generates accuracy radars, time-per-question metrics, topic mastery heatmaps, and weak-area diagnostic alerts.

Can I listen to Lofi study music while practising?

Yes. Quant-Elite features a built-in ambient Lofi study audio player with curated tracks to help maintain deep focus during long practice sessions.

You can use the built-in Feedback page or in-app support screen to suggest new chapters, report content errors, request resources, or share suggestions for future updates.
