Account Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's data safety policies, we provide a way for you to request the deletion of your account and all associated data.

Important: Deleting your account is a permanent action. All your coins, progress, exam history, and AI mentor interactions will be erased and cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In-App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Quant Elite app.
  2. Open the side menu (Drawer).
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account and data will be removed instantly.

Option 2: Web Request

If you no longer have the app installed, please email us at:

theuniquedawg29@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your account and the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 48 hours.
